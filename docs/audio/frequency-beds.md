# Original soft frequency beds

Created 19 October 2026 with `scripts/generate-frequency-beds.mjs`.
The project dedicates these original synthesized recordings to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), including
commercial use. No external recordings, samples, religious audio or provider
calls are used.

Exactly three choices: 432 Hz, 528 Hz and 963 Hz. Each uses its named carrier,
a quieter lower octave and soft harmonics, with a subtle 24-second envelope.
These are ambient listening preferences, not claims of ADHD treatment or
special healing properties. They are mono sounds, not binaural beats.

Files are 44.1 kHz / 16-bit PCM WAV, 24 seconds, 2,116,844 bytes each. Integer
cycle counts and periodic envelopes preserve continuity at the loop boundary.
Existing gesture gating, individual saved volumes and background playback apply.
No new source-link UI or additional control is added.
