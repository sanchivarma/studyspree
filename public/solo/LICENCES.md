Each MP4 is silent H.264, up to 8 MB. Filmed shots are cropped to a constant 16:9 frame, encoded without optical-flow interpolation, and joined by a 600 ms seam dissolve. The black-hole source is slowed four times using temporal blending; Earth retains its original slow rotation. Desks, window frames and the two layered-scene stills are original generated artwork, with source prompts in docs/solo-scenes/prompts.json.

The aquarium footage is slowed twice using temporal frame blending. Selected loops rotate their playback start into uninterrupted footage; the source-end dissolve occurs inside the loop.
