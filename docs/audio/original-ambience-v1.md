# Original StudySpree ambience v1

Created on 17 October 2026 by `scripts/generate-default-media.mjs` for this project.

- `rain-v1.webm`: seeded broadband noise with a filtered low-frequency bed.
- `city-night-v1.webm`: filtered noise with a soft, slowly modulated hum.
- `evening-outdoor-v1.webm`: wind-like noise and synthesized insect-like tones.
- `keyboard-room-v1.webm`: synthesized, decaying key taps over a soft room bed.
- `still-water-v1.webm`: an original sustained C/E/G ambient chord, without a third-party melody or recording.

All sounds are synthesized locally from mathematical signals and seeded noise. No microphone, samples, external libraries of sounds, downloaded audio, or streaming services are involved. These are stylized soundscapes, not field recordings. They are encoded as stereo Opus/WebM and crossfaded by the existing ambience engine. Default playback remains off.
