# Silent placeholder provenance

Recorded date: 2026-10-14.

The four `.webm` files named in `public/audio/LICENCES.md` are identical copies
of original six-second synthetic silence. `scripts/generate-silent-audio.mjs`
encodes a constant zero signal at 48 kHz with Chromium's Opus/WebM encoder.
It does not access a microphone, camera, external recording, or streaming site.

These silent placeholder assets are dedicated under CC0-1.0; commercial app
use is permitted. No third-party audio or licence certificate is involved.
This statement covers these placeholders; it does not license future music.

The existing `.gitkeep` file is an empty directory marker, with no recording or
creative content. Its ledger entry records that no audio licence is applicable.

The music catalogue temporarily reuses `rain.webm` as a labelled silent preview.
Real replacements need their own source, licence, date, commercial permission,
and certificate entry. Ship them under new filenames because audio is cached
for one year as immutable.
