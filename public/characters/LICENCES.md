# Character asset provenance

These eight fictional adult study partners are original procedural vector illustrations and animations authored for StudySpree. They use no external photographs, downloaded artwork, trained character models, fonts, or real-person likenesses. Each GIF has 32 original frames, a 4.8-second infinite loop, and a matching static poster. They are illustrated companions, not live camera feeds or conversational AI.
